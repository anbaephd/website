About Me

Anders Launer Bæk-Petersen is a PhD Fellow in Software Engineering at the University of Southern Denmark (SDU). His research studies how to make machine learning systems reliable in production, from a software engineering and software architecture point of view, with a focus on operational metrics, self-adaptive and self-managing architectures, and scalable AI pipelines.

Anders brings industry experience as an ML engineer at Energinet and a data scientist at Esoft, and holds an MSc in Mathematical Modeling and Computation from DTU and a BSc in Robot Systems from SDU. During his doctoral studies, Anders has been a teaching assistant in the SDU Big Data and Data Science Technologies course and has supervised more than 50 BSc and BEng engineering students on their final projects.
