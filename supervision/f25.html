  1. At latest 31/01/2025 - Upload your TEK Bachelorprojektkontrakt here spoc.sdu.dk/form/1216
  2. February 1st: The project period starts here or when your TEK Bachelorprojektkontrakt has been approved.
  3. At latest 02/06/2025 - Upload your Bachelorprojektrapport here digitaleksamen.sdu.dk
