Examination Format

☝️ The SDU TEK Education has final authority over the format of the examination. The structure presented here must serve as a guide.

Structure for Individual Examination

BSc

  • 5-7 min: Presentation of own topic within your project.
  • 15-20 min: Dialog and questions with an offset in the project report
  • 5 min: Assessment and awarding of your marks

BEng

  • 10-15 min: Presentation of own topic within your project.
  • 25-30 min: Dialog and questions with an offset in the project report
  • 5 min: Assessment and awarding of your marks
